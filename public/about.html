A room plan that starts with what you already have.

Second Room is a working, browser-based prototype for planning furniture reuse. It makes a simple decision visible: keep an item, repair it, buy a replacement, or leave it out. The 3D room and the action checklist stay connected.

Why reuse?

Keeping products useful for longer is part of a circular economy. The European Environment Agency identifies repair, refurbishment, repurposing and redistribution as ways to extend product lifetimes. Read the EEA overview.

The product hypothesis is that seeing existing furniture in a future room and attaching a concrete action makes reuse easier to plan. This prototype has not yet been evaluated in a field study. No school partnership, customer adoption or measured waste reduction is claimed.

How the figures work

  • Reuse planned counts items with the Reuse action. Repair items are listed separately in the checklist.
  • Budget needed adds replacement prices for Buy new items and repair budgets for Repair items. Reuse and Leave out cost zero in this simplified model.
  • Replacement baseline adds the entered replacement price for every included item. Leaving an item out removes it from both the baseline and the plan.
  • Budget difference is the baseline minus the plan. It can be negative. These are user inputs, not verified retail prices, actual savings or environmental measurements.
  • Confirmed reused counts reuse or repair decisions you explicitly mark completed. It is self-reported and is cleared when the item's action changes.

What the prototype does and does not do

Five original procedural furniture objects, two room arrangements, camera orbit and zoom, object selection, bounded movement, rotation, decisions, editable prices, notes, condition-based suggestions, completion tracking and portable JSON plans work in the browser. “Leave out” removes the item from the 3D view. The checklist can be downloaded as text.

The objects and prices are illustrative examples. This is not a CAD, collision, structural, electrical-safety or professional repair assessment. Inspect real items and dimensions before use. Electrical repairs require a qualified professional. No carbon footprint or kilograms-of-waste calculation is offered.

Privacy and access

No account, backend, tracking or payment is required. The current plan is stored in this browser's local storage. Reset restores the example. Export saves a JSON file; Import reads a local Second Room plan of up to 64 KB. Clearing site data removes the saved plan. Google Fonts may receive standard font requests; system fonts work if that service is unavailable.
